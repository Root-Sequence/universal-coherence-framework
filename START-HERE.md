## In one sentence

The Universal Coherence Framework is an exploratory, falsifiable cross-domain model for comparing patterns of instability, constraint, adaptation, and coordination.

## If you remember only three things

1. **Coherence is not conformity.** Dissent, autonomy, boundaries, slack, and plurality may be necessary.
2. Cross-domain similarity creates a question to test, not evidence of one universal mechanism.
3. Established evidence, framework hypotheses, and interpretation must stay separate.

## Current reality

UCF currently offers working terms, a four-state vocabulary—Chaos, Tension, Flow, Unity—models, examples, and methodological boundaries. It is active and revisable. The labels are analytical shorthand, not verified stages every system follows, moral rankings, clinical diagnoses, or permission to centralize control.

## Where it fits

UCF is one optional analytical framework inside Root Sequence. Other projects may test, adapt, critique, or reject it. It does not own ethics, governance, human experience, intelligence, or the conclusions of the projects that use it.

## What to do next

- **Learning the vocabulary:** read the [glossary](docs/glossary.md).
- **Evaluating credibility:** read [claims](docs/claims.md) beside [evidence](docs/evidence.md).
- **Applying UCF:** define the domain, observable variables, limits, counterexamples, and what would make the comparison fail.
- **Disagreeing:** preserve the counterexample; forcing a fit makes the framework less useful.
