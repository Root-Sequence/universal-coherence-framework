# UCF claims register

**Revision:** 2026-09-16 draft; AI-assisted; author review pending. Types and editorial statuses follow [evidence.md](evidence.md). Internal documents provide provenance, not independent confirmation. These IDs are separate from the living paper's C-series.

## UC-001 — Functional fit needs an assessment boundary

**Type / status:** WORKING DEFINITION / proposed.  
**Claim:** Examine fit among assumptions, behavior, dependencies, purposes, and operating conditions, with stated boundaries and timescales.  
**Basis:** v1's four layers, earlier glossary, and Root Sequence's model/reality framing; reconciled in [v1.1 draft](../models/ucf-model-v1.1-draft.md).  
**Limit / objection:** This could rename an ordinary systems review; it is not operationalized merely by being defined.  
**Revision test:** Reviewers should identify the boundary and observations without substituting personal approval. Discard terminology that adds no clarity.  
**Paper links:** C-003, C-012.

## UC-002 — A local objective is not an all-effects guarantee

**Type / status:** MATHEMATICAL DEMONSTRATION / supported within the constructed example's scope.  
**Claim:** In the [booking example](../models/examples/booking-service/README.md), maximizing completion count can exclude longer requests; another rule redistributes access without removing unmet work.  
**Basis:** Deterministic enumeration and implementation checks.  
**Limit / objection:** This proves a property of the chosen model, not prevalence or a socially preferred policy. A target with explicit coverage constraints blocks that particular exclusion; sufficient capacity removes the conflict in this fixture.  
**Revision test:** Publish assumptions and cases where the result disappears. Reassess any application that changes durations, priorities, or service outcomes.  
**Paper links:** C-001, C-005, C-011.

## UC-003 — Cross-domain transfer is a separate hypothesis

**Type / status:** HYPOTHESIS / proposed.  
**Claim:** Some structural comparisons may support useful questions in multiple domains.  
**Basis:** The existing framework's research ambition, not a universal finding.  
**Limit / objection:** Similar labels may hide different mechanisms or incompatible measures.  
**Revision test:** Specify mappings and alternatives in at least two domains; retain transfer failures. No current example validates cross-domain comparability.  
**Paper links:** C-012.

## UC-004 — State labels may add information beyond the layers

**Type / status:** HYPOTHESIS / unresolved.  
**Claim:** Chaos, Tension, Flow, and Unity may distinguish locally observable patterns.  
**Basis:** Earlier overview; first editorial application in the booking example.  
**Limit / objection:** That application found no established added explanation from the labels. No independent raters participated.  
**Revision test:** Compare pre-specified local definitions against ordinary descriptions with the same information. Narrow, rename, or omit labels if they remain outcome-dependent gloss.  
**Paper links:** C-003, C-012.

## UC-005 — Functional coherence establishes moral legitimacy

**Type / status:** INFERENCE / retired as an unqualified assertion.  
**Historical source:** Glossary formulations of harm and fake coherence risked conflating function with approval.  
**Revision:** Assess purposes, authority, harms, and objections separately; do not assume an effective coercive arrangement must fail to explain objections to it.  
**Limit / objection:** Functional and normative descriptions influence each other; separating questions is not proving independence.  
**Revision test:** Use the contrasting fictional cases in the worked example; seek disagreement and circular definitions.  
**Paper links:** C-003, C-004, C-008.

## UC-006 — Compression necessarily improves coherence and reduces entropy

**Type / status:** CROSS-DOMAIN GENERALIZATION / retired as an unqualified assertion.  
**Historical source:** Earlier glossary, Compression and Entropy entries.  
**Revision:** Distinguish coding, lossy summarization, information entropy, and physical entropy.  
**Basis:** Shannon and the explicit mathematical examples in [evidence.md](evidence.md).  
**Limit / objection:** Compression can still help a specified task. The correction rejects an unconditional guarantee, not all compression.  
**Revision test:** State the variable, information retained, error criterion, and task before claiming benefit.  
**Paper links:** C-005.

## UC-007 — Fundamental symmetry implies universal forward accumulation of coherence

**Type / status:** PHYSICAL / SYSTEMIC INFERENCE / retired as an unqualified assertion.  
**Historical source:** Earlier time essay.  
**Revision:** Separate invertibility, T-symmetry, retrodiction, and experience. BABAR reports T violation; it does not settle invertibility or explain all temporal asymmetries. No forward-accumulation law is supplied.  
**Limit / objection:** Conditional reversible models and useful historical reconstruction remain possible; neither is ruled out by this correction.  
**Revision test:** Give exact physical assumptions and an independent measure before extending a time claim to UCF.  
**Paper links:** C-007, C-011.

## UC-008 — Coherence explains consciousness or establishes an artificial entity

**Type / status:** INTERPRETATION / SPECULATION / unresolved, not an established foundation.  
**Historical source:** Glossary and speculative vocabulary.  
**Revision:** Retain the questions without treating coordination or self-modeling as a test of experience, identity, or moral status.  
**Limit / objection:** Rejecting the inference does not prove artificial experience absent. UCF has no validated measure here.  
**Revision test:** Engage competing domain theories and explicit evidence before making an experiential claim.  
**Paper links:** C-009's distinction between capability and other conclusions.

## UC-009 — Conditions can alter which behavior remains viable

**Type / status:** HYPOTHESIS / proposed.  
**Basis:** Root Sequence's public Intelligence Ecology concept; the booking fixture illustrates one constrained choice problem, not behavior learned over time.  
**Limit / objection:** Selection, adaptation, and changed measurement can be confused; incentives do not determine every action.  
**Revision test:** A bounded causal comparison with competing explanations, non-AI alternatives, and end-to-end burdens. No such empirical test has been performed here.  
**Paper links:** C-002, C-006, C-009.

## UC-010 — Improvement is a situated comparison

**Type / status:** WORKING DEFINITION / proposed.  
**Claim:** Name what improves, for whom, over which horizon, and what worsens or remains uncertain.  
**Basis:** Recent public project framing and the model reconciliation.  
**Limit / objection:** This may yield incomparable outcomes; do not force an overall score. It is neither a theorem against a final coherent state nor a promise of monotonic progress.  
**Revision test:** Retain mixed outcomes and failed repairs. Examine whether the language hides rather than explains uncertainty.  
**Paper links:** C-006, C-007, C-011.
