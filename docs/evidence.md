# Evidence convention and source log

**Started:** 2026-09-16. This is a proposed editorial standard, not a completed literature review.

## Classify each consequential claim

Use **DOMAIN RESULT**, **MATHEMATICAL DEMONSTRATION**, **WORKING DEFINITION**, **HYPOTHESIS**, **STRUCTURAL ANALOGY**, **INTERPRETATION / NORMATIVE COMMITMENT**, or **SPECULATION**. A document may contain several kinds. Fiction and simulations require explicit labels and assumptions.

Record the claim ID, domain and boundary, source and exact version where available, reviewed portions, limitations, alternative explanations, counterexamples, and what would lead to revision. A source may justify one sentence without supporting the extrapolation beside it.

Editorial status is separate: **proposed**, **supported within stated scope**, **contested**, **superseded**, or **retired as an unqualified assertion**. Do not assign numerical confidence to a definition or convert repetition into evidence.

## An information-theory correction

For a discrete variable, Shannon entropy can be written `H(X) = -sum(p(x) log2 p(x))`. An invertible relabeling preserves its probabilities and therefore its entropy. Encoding can change expected description length without making the source less uncertain. Replacing every message with the same symbol instead produces zero output entropy while deleting all distinctions. These are mathematical observations under the stated discrete model, not empirical findings about people.

Consequently, “less entropy” and “more compression” are not general certificates of better understanding or healthier coordination. Claims involving thermodynamic entropy need their own physical model and accounting; this review does not supply one for society.

## Research and participant boundaries

A constructed model can establish consequences of chosen assumptions. It cannot estimate prevalence in a service, prove a universal framework, or confer legitimacy on the chosen rule. Independent review and human-participant research are different from an AI-assisted editorial exercise.

Before a participant study, specify consent, data minimization, accessibility, withdrawal, burdens, and oversight appropriate to the study. Repository contributions or community membership do not constitute research consent. Do not gather intimate life histories merely to populate a coherence dashboard.
