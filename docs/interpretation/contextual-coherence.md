# Contextual coherence: interpreting differences without ranking people

**Claim type:** INTERPRETATION / NORMATIVE COMMITMENT + proposed analytical mapping<br>
**Status:** AI-assisted / author review pending / untested (2026-10-29)<br>
**Canonical source:** Root Sequence's [Coherence Without Uniformity / Contextual Coherence, PR #57](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/pull/57), pinned to [source commit 3ab2753](https://github.com/Root-Sequence/root-sequence/blob/3ab27534c555185bcdde9467356587ea9b5a05a2/research/contextual-coherence/README.md). The principle and source ledger remain there. This page maintains only UCF's optional translation; local review may accept, narrow, defer, or reject it independently.

The source distinguishes shared protections from locally responsive operational rules, with participation, interoperable interfaces, and accountable revision. UCF can help formulate a bounded relationship map. It cannot certify a rule's morality, legitimacy, safety, or legal authority, or decide that a locality must adopt it.

## Mapping to the proposed v1.1 layers

Use the [proposed v1.1 model](../../models/ucf-model-v1.1-draft.md) as a vocabulary to test, with domain variables defined before labels:

| Layer | Possible question in a connected-place rule comparison | Example observations to define locally |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Internal | Are an institution's stated protection and operative decisions consistent within the specified boundary? | Binding rule text, actual authorized actions, exceptions, and resources committed |
| Inter-agent | Can differently situated actors understand obligations, influence decisions, and challenge burdens? | Comprehension errors, access to appeal, unresolved objections, and decision-power distribution |
| Systemic | How do local arrangements interact with shared infrastructure, ecological constraints, and other jurisdictions? | Displaced harms, boundary failures, maintenance dependencies, and resource inequality |
| Temporal | Can the arrangement maintain protection through change and implement justified revisions? | Rule versions, reasons, enacted amendments, review delay, repair, and persistent consequences |

These are candidate observations for one social-design application. They are neither validated measures of the layers nor proxies for personal worth. Internal consistency can describe an oppressive institution; successful coordination can serve a harmful purpose. The ethical and authority judgments remain separately argued.

## What must remain distinct

**Coherence and uniformity.** Different procedures may support compatible relationships; identical procedures may fail differently situated people. Whether either works is an empirical and normative inquiry with separate questions. The optional state labels must not turn greater sameness into a preferred developmental stage.

**Dissent and failure.** A challenge may reveal an omission, competing value, false belief, procedural defect, or unjust power. Record which is at issue. Do not code dissent, refusal, cultural difference, non-participation, or local autonomy as Chaos or Tension merely because they obstruct agreement.

**Capability and authority.** Technical ability to rewrite a rule, a favorable modeled outcome, or a UCF description does not authorize a protection waiver or confer consent. Modification powers and their own amendment process need an independently specified governance basis.

**Restoration and repair.** Returning a rule to an earlier version does not erase injuries, lost access, disclosure, resource depletion, or changed dependencies. Temporal analysis must retain relevant history rather than equate reversibility with a successful reset.

## A bounded test of added value

Compare an ordinary competent institutional/design review with the same source packet plus this four-layer mapping. Give both conditions equal evidence, time, domain expertise, access perspectives, and evaluation criteria. Ask whether the mapping identifies actionable omissions, displaced burdens, authority gaps, or revision failures that the baseline misses—and whether it increases confusion or administrative work.

Include a well-performing common rule and a harmful local variation as counterexamples. Improvements should be traced to specific observations and decisions rather than the presence of UCF terminology. If the ordinary review performs equally well with less burden, the mapping may add no useful value in this setting. The proposed comparison has not been run; participant evaluation requires a separate protocol.

The source's Wiggle illustration is not UCF validation. This translation adds no traffic evidence, model equation, universal score, new state, claim-series result, or release. Apply the [evidence convention](../evidence.md); record failed mappings and keep ethical commitments separate from descriptive observations.
